  • Title: Women in STEM: Power, Culture, and Accountability in Physics
    Date:Thursday, 5 March 2026
    Time: 11:00 - 12:00
    Host: Sohini Dutta, PhD student, Jodrell Bank Centre for Astrophysics, The University of Manchester
    Description: Women remain underrepresented at senior levels in Physics and Astronomy, despite decades of initiatives aimed at improving gender balance. Why does this persist? And what role do culture, power structures, and institutional accountability play?
    This interactive session will explore the realities of women’s experiences in STEM, from structural career design and meritocracy myths to representation, intersectionality, and the impact of harassment and power abuse in academic environments. Drawing on UK data and lived experience, we will reflect on what has changed, what has not, and what meaningful accountability looks like in 2026.
    This will be a discussion-led session with space for reflection, shared experience, and constructive thinking about how departments can build safer, fairer research cultures.
