  • Title: A chat with Claire Davies
    Date:Thursday, 4 February 2026
    Time: 11:00 - 12:00
    Speaker: Dr Claire Davies, award-winning astrophysicist and LGBTQ+ advocate, University of Exeter
    About Claire: Dr Claire Davies is a Senior Lecturer in Physics & Astronomy at the University of Exeter and the founder of the PRISM Exeter network for LGBTQ+ STEMM professionals and students across Southwest England. She has an astronomy research background – focused on star and planetary system formation – but jumped ship to a teaching-track academic career in 2022 and now focuses on inclusive education.
    Description: I will present an overview of my career journey to date, with its ups and – perhaps more importantly for a forum such as this - its downs. I will particularly explore themes of navigating academia while queer, research culture, and inclusive education. I will aim to touch on these subjects rather than to deep dive into them so that a variety of topics are available to pick up for discussion following the talk.
