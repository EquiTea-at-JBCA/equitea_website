  • Title: Welcome Back to EquiTea! What Do You Want From Us This Year?
    Description: Kicking off another new academic year of EquiTea! For anyone new to the department (or new to us), EquiTea is our monthly forum for discussing equity, diversity and inclusion in physics and astronomy, open to everyone, staff and students alike, regardless of background.
    This session is a little different. Instead of our usual focused discussion session format, we're starting the year by handing the floor to you. We'll briefly introduce who we are and what we do, then spend the rest of the session finding out what you'd like EquiTea to cover, discuss, and be this year, with pens, post-its, and a big shared board to build together. Come with your questions, frustrations, hopes, and ideas; leave having helped shape the year ahead.
    Just yourself, and your thoughts on whatever you'd like to see more (or less) of.
    Date:Thursday, 08 October 2026
    Time: 11:00 - 12:00
    Location: Lovell Room, Alan Turing Building
    Hosts: Sohini Dutta and Tobias Russell, PhD students, Jodrell Bank Center for Astrophysics, The University of Manchester
