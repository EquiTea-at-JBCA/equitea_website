  • Title: Over a year on from the Supreme Court ruling – what are the current state of trans rights in the UK and what can we do about it?
    Speaker: Tobias Russell
    Date: Thursday, 04 June 2026
    Time: 11:00 - 12:00
    Abstract: It has now been over a year since the Supreme Court ruling and the EHRC Interim Update were issued. In this talk I will go through what this means for trans people in the UK and walk through a timeline summarising recent developments in the reversal of trans rights and freedoms in the UK. I will discuss what is being done to fight this and what you can do to help on a departmental and university level and beyond.
