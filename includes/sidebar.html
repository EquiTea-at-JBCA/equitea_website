  • Title: Pride and Prejudice - the story of Physicist Émilie du Châtelet
    Description: As a young woman Émilie du Châtelet, member of 18th century France's higher nobility, developed great scientific ambitions. Her love affair with poet-philosopher Voltaire helped her realize many of them, but may also have contributed to her unjust fall into relative scientific oblivion afterwards. This talk highlights her achievements and influence on the spread of the scientific revolution in Europe.
    Date: Thursday, 05 November 2026
    Time: 11:00 - 12:00
    Location: Lovell Room, Alan Turing Building
    Speaker: Jan-Willem van Holten, from the University of Leiden
